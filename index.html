How to play
  1. Click a shell to hide the pea under it — that starts a new game.
  2. Press Shuffle: the shells swap places for 5 seconds.
  3. Click the shell you think hides the pea. Keep guessing until you find it.
  4. Only a find on the first guess counts as a win.

Keyboard: 1 2 3 pick the left, middle and right shell, S shuffles.
