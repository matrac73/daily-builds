Not a target

Cracking here only makes sparks. The whip only types into editable zones: <textarea>, text <input> and contenteditable elements.

  • Short, sharp flicks crack — slow waving does not.
  • 3 hits in a row → LOUDER! · 6 hits → MAXIMUM!!!
  • Esc puts the whip away.

Take it to any website

Drag this bookmarklet to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any page to get the whip there (pages with a strict Content-Security-Policy may block it).
